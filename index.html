How it works

Laundry day in four easy steps

Bring your clothes and detergent. We supply the machines.

Load

Pick a washer that fits your load, from everyday clothes to comforters.

Pay & wash

Choose your cycle, start the machine, and relax while it runs.

Dry

Move everything to a dryer. Drying is free with your wash.

Fold & go

Use our folding tables and head home with fresh, clean laundry.
