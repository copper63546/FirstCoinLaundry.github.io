How it works

Laundry day in four easy steps

Bring your clothes and detergent. We supply the machines.

Load

Pick a washer that fits your load. Fill it only up to 2/3 full, and half full is best.

Pay & wash

Choose your cycle, start the machine, and relax while it runs.

Dry

Move everything to a dryer. Drying is free with your wash.

Fold & go

Use our folding tables and head home with fresh, clean laundry.

Caution

Don't overload the washer

Clothes need room to tumble. An overloaded washer doesn't clean well, can leave soap behind, and is hard on the machine. If it's too full, split it into two loads or use a bigger washer.

  • Half full: best clean
  • 2/3 full: the most you should load
