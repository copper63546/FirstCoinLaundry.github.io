Text message program

Wash & Fold Text Updates

Know when your wash & fold order is ready without calling the store.

What you'll get

When you drop off a wash & fold order at First Coin Laundry, we can text you about that order:

  • A confirmation that we received your order
  • A message when your order is ready for pickup
  • A reminder if your order is still waiting for pickup
  • Replies to questions you text us about your order

These texts are only about your order. We do not send marketing or promotional messages through this program.

How to sign up

  1. When you drop off a wash & fold order at 4877 Memorial Dr, Stone Mountain, GA 30083, give the attendant your mobile number and ask for text updates.
  2. We send you one text asking if you want updates. It includes links to our Privacy Policy and SMS Terms.
  3. Reply YES to agree and opt in. You'll get a text confirming you're signed up.

This is the sign-up text you'll receive:

First Coin Laundry: Reply YES to get text updates about your wash & fold orders. Msg frequency varies, usually 1-3 msgs per order. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out. Privacy: https://copper63546.github.io/FirstCoinLaundry.github.io/privacy.html Terms: https://copper63546.github.io/FirstCoinLaundry.github.io/sms-terms.html

By replying YES, you agree to receive text updates about your wash & fold orders from First Coin Laundry at that number. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Consent is not a condition of purchase. See our SMS Terms and Privacy Policy.

If you don't reply YES, you won't get any more texts from us. Texts are optional. We'll still wash your order, and you can call us at (404) 330-8260 to check on it.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies by order, usually 1 to 3 messages for each wash & fold order. Message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile plan.

Text keywords

YESReply to our sign-up text to agree and opt in.
STOPStop all texts from this program. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed.
HELPGet our contact information and help with the program.
STARTTurn texts back on after you've opted out.

You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT to stop messages.

Your privacy

We use your mobile number only to send updates about your orders. We do not sell or share your mobile number or text messaging opt-in information with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
