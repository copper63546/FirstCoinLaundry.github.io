1. Program description

The Program sends text messages about wash & fold orders you drop off at our store. Messages may include order confirmations, ready-for-pickup notices, pickup reminders, and replies to questions you send us about your order. The Program does not send marketing or promotional messages.

2. How you opt in

When you drop off a wash & fold order, you may give us your mobile number and ask for text updates. We will send one text asking you to confirm, with links to these terms and our Privacy Policy. You opt in by replying YES to that text. If you do not reply YES, we will not send you any more Program messages. Opting in is optional and is not a condition of any purchase or service.

3. Message frequency

Message frequency varies by order, usually 1 to 3 messages per wash & fold order.

4. Cost

Message and data rates may apply. Any charges are billed by your mobile carrier, according to your plan. We do not charge for Program messages.

5. How to opt out

You can cancel at any time. Reply STOP to any message from us. You can also reply UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END or QUIT. After you send STOP, we will send one message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and you will receive no further Program messages. To join again, reply START or sign up again at the store.

6. Help

10. Changing your number

If you change or give up your mobile number, reply STOP from that number first, or tell us at the store, so we don't text the new owner of that number.
