Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how First Coin Laundry Inc. ("First Coin Laundry", "we", "us"), 4877 Memorial Dr, Stone Mountain, GA 30083, collects, uses and protects information about customers who use our store, our website, and our Wash & Fold Text Updates program.

1. Information we collect

  • Contact information you give us, such as your name and mobile phone number, for example on a wash & fold drop-off form.
  • Order information, such as your order number, drop-off and pickup dates, and any instructions you give us.
  • Text message records, such as when you opted in, the messages we send you, your replies, and opt-out requests.
  • Website information. Our website does not have accounts or forms and does not use advertising trackers. Our website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and to keep the site running.

2. How we use your information

  • To process your wash & fold orders and contact you about them
  • To send the text message updates you asked for
  • To answer your questions and provide customer service
  • To keep records of your consent and honor opt-out requests
  • To comply with the law and protect our business and customers

3. Text messaging and your mobile number

If you opt in to our Wash & Fold Text Updates program, we use your mobile number only to send messages about your orders. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. See our SMS Terms & Conditions.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the categories of sharing described in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers who help us run our business, such as our text messaging provider, only so they can deliver messages for us. They may not use your information for their own purposes.
  • For legal reasons, when required by law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of our customers, our business or others.
  • In a business transfer, such as a sale of the business, where this policy would continue to apply to your information.

5. How long we keep information

We keep order and messaging records only as long as needed to provide our services, keep a record of your consent and opt-out choices, and meet legal requirements.

6. How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices

  • Stop texts: reply STOP to any message.
  • Access or correct your information: call us or ask at the store.
  • Delete your information: ask us, and we will delete it unless we need to keep it for legal reasons.

8. Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated policy on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
